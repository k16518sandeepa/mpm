Placeholders: {title} {tagline} {type} {year} {genres} {platforms} {developer} {publisher} {modes} {language} {version} {size} {cert} {score} {story} {minimum} {recommended}. A line whose placeholders are all empty is dropped, so unused details never leave blank labels. {minimum} and {recommended} expand to a whole block, or nothing when empty. Lines starting with > become quotes in the HTML copy. Saved in this browser only. To change it for the whole team, edit DEFAULT_TEMPLATE at the top of the script.
